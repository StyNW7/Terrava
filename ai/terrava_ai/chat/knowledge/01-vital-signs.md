# Tanda vital normal sapi / Normal cattle vital signs

## Suhu tubuh normal
Suhu tubuh normal sapi dewasa adalah 38,0–39,3 °C. Pedet sedikit lebih tinggi (38,5–39,5 °C). Suhu di atas 39,5 °C dianggap demam; di atas 40,5 °C demam tinggi dan perlu dokter hewan segera. Suhu di bawah 37,5 °C (hipotermia) bisa menandakan syok, milk fever (hipokalsemia), atau sensor ear tag yang tidak menempel dengan baik. Suhu naik sedikit pada sore hari dan saat cuaca panas, jadi Terrava membandingkan suhu dengan baseline masing-masing ternak.

## Normal body temperature
Adult cattle normally run 38.0–39.3 °C; calves slightly higher (38.5–39.5 °C). Above 39.5 °C is a fever; above 40.5 °C is a high fever that needs a veterinarian quickly. Below 37.5 °C (hypothermia) can mean shock, milk fever (hypocalcaemia) or an ear tag sensor that is not seated properly. Temperature rises a little in the afternoon and in hot weather, which is why Terrava compares every animal with its own baseline.

## Detak jantung dan saturasi oksigen
Detak jantung istirahat sapi dewasa 48–84 kali per menit; pedet 100–140. Detak di atas 100 bpm saat istirahat menandakan stres, nyeri, demam, atau gangguan jantung-paru. Saturasi oksigen (SpO2) normal 95–100%. SpO2 di bawah 92% mengarah ke gangguan pernapasan seperti pneumonia atau Bovine Respiratory Disease (BRD).

## Heart rate and oxygen saturation
Resting heart rate is 48–84 bpm for adult cattle and 100–140 for calves. Above 100 bpm at rest points to stress, pain, fever or a heart-lung problem. Normal oxygen saturation (SpO2) is 95–100%; below 92% suggests respiratory disease such as pneumonia or Bovine Respiratory Disease (BRD).

## Aktivitas / Activity
Indeks aktivitas Terrava (0–100) dihitung dari akselerometer dan giroskop ear tag. Setiap ternak punya pola harian: aktif pagi dan sore, tenang di malam hari. Penurunan aktivitas lebih dari 30% dari baseline 7 hari adalah tanda awal sakit, pincang, atau stres panas. Lonjakan aktivitas 60–150% di atas baseline pada sapi betina biasanya menandakan birahi.

The Terrava activity index (0–100) comes from the ear tag accelerometer and gyroscope. Each animal has a daily rhythm: active in the morning and late afternoon, calm at night. A drop of more than 30% below the 7-day baseline is an early sign of illness, lameness or heat stress. A 60–150% surge above baseline in a cow usually means she is in heat.

## Skor kesehatan Terrava / Terrava health score
Skor 0–100 menggabungkan model AI (probabilitas kondisi normal) dan jarak tanda vital dari rentang normal. 80–100 = Sehat, 58–79 = Perlu perhatian, di bawah 58 = Kritis. Ternak tanpa data terbaru ditandai Tidak aktif.

The 0–100 score blends the AI model (probability the animal is in a normal state) with how far each vital sign sits from its normal range. 80–100 = Healthy, 58–79 = Needs attention, below 58 = Critical. Animals without recent data are marked Inactive.

# Heat stress dan THI / Heat stress and THI

## Apa itu THI
Temperature-Humidity Index (THI) menggabungkan suhu udara dan kelembapan: THI = 0,8 × T + (RH/100) × (T − 14,4) + 46,4. Pita risiko untuk sapi: < 72 normal, 72–79 ringan, 80–89 sedang, ≥ 90 berat. Di Indonesia, THI siang hari sering melewati 78 terutama di dataran rendah, sehingga sapi perah Friesian Holstein sangat rentan.

## What THI means
The Temperature-Humidity Index combines air temperature and humidity: THI = 0.8 × T + (RH/100) × (T − 14.4) + 46.4. Cattle risk bands: below 72 normal, 72–79 mild, 80–89 moderate, 90 and above severe. In Indonesia daytime THI often exceeds 78, especially in the lowlands, so Friesian Holstein dairy cows are very vulnerable.

## Dampak heat stress
Nafsu makan turun, produksi susu turun sekitar 0,2–0,4 kg per ekor per hari untuk setiap kenaikan 1 poin THI di atas ~69, kesuburan menurun, risiko kematian meningkat pada heat stress berat. Tanda: napas terengah-engah dengan mulut terbuka, air liur berlebih, suhu tubuh > 39,5 °C, ternak berkumpul di tempat teduh.

Effects: appetite falls, milk yield drops about 0.2–0.4 kg per cow per day for every THI point above ~69, fertility declines, and mortality rises in severe episodes. Signs: open-mouth panting, drooling, body temperature above 39.5 °C, animals crowding into shade.

## Cara mengatasi / How to respond
- Air minum bersih dan dingin tanpa batas (sapi perah 80–120 liter/hari) / Unlimited clean, cool water (dairy cows 80–120 L/day).
- Kipas dan ventilasi; kombinasikan dengan sprinkler untuk pendinginan evaporatif / Fans and ventilation; combine with sprinklers for evaporative cooling.
- Peneduh di area terbuka, atap berinsulasi / Shade in open areas and insulated roofing.
- Beri pakan pada pagi dan sore yang sejuk; tambah elektrolit / Feed in the cool morning and evening; add electrolytes.
- Hindari penanganan, transportasi, dan vaksinasi saat siang panas / Avoid handling, transport and vaccination in the midday heat.

## Fitur Terrava terkait
Sensor lingkungan di kandang menghitung THI otomatis dan memunculkan alert "Suhu kandang di luar batas nyaman". Prakiraan cuaca dipakai untuk alert "Prediksi gelombang panas besok" sehingga peternak bisa bersiap sehari sebelumnya.

Barn environmental sensors compute THI automatically and raise a "Barn temperature outside comfort range" alert. The weather forecast drives the "Heat wave forecast for tomorrow" alert so farmers can prepare a day ahead.

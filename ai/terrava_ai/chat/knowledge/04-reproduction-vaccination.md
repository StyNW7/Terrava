# Reproduksi dan vaksinasi / Reproduction and vaccination

## Deteksi birahi (estrus)
Siklus birahi sapi rata-rata 21 hari (18–24), birahi berlangsung 12–18 jam. Tanda: gelisah, aktivitas naik tajam, menaiki atau diam dinaiki sapi lain, lendir bening dari vulva. Ear tag Terrava mendeteksi lonjakan aktivitas ≥ 1,6× baseline selama ≥ 3 jam. Waktu inseminasi buatan (IB) terbaik adalah 12–18 jam setelah awal birahi (aturan pagi–sore: birahi pagi → IB sore; birahi sore → IB pagi berikutnya).

## Estrus detection
The bovine oestrous cycle averages 21 days (18–24) and heat lasts 12–18 hours. Signs: restlessness, a sharp rise in activity, mounting or standing to be mounted, clear mucus. The Terrava ear tag detects an activity surge of at least 1.6× baseline lasting 3 hours or more. The best time for artificial insemination is 12–18 hours after heat onset (AM–PM rule: seen in the morning → inseminate that evening; seen in the evening → next morning).

## Kebuntingan dan melahirkan / Pregnancy and calving
Lama bunting sapi sekitar 280–285 hari. Menjelang melahirkan aktivitas sering turun dan ternak memisahkan diri. Pantau ketat 0–3 hari setelah melahirkan untuk milk fever, retensi plasenta, dan metritis.

Gestation lasts about 280–285 days. Activity often falls and the cow isolates herself before calving. Watch closely for 0–3 days after calving for milk fever, retained placenta and metritis.

## Jadwal vaksinasi umum di Indonesia
Ikuti arahan dinas peternakan / dokter hewan setempat. Umumnya:
- PMK (Penyakit Mulut dan Kuku): dosis awal, booster 4 minggu kemudian, lalu ulangan tiap 6 bulan.
- LSD (Lumpy Skin Disease): satu dosis, ulangan tahunan.
- SE (Septicaemia Epizootica / ngorok): tahunan, terutama sebelum musim hujan.
- Antraks: tahunan di daerah endemis.
- Brucellosis: pada sapi betina muda sesuai program pemerintah.
Catat setiap vaksinasi di tab Health Records Terrava; status vaksinasi akan berubah menjadi "Jatuh tempo" atau "Terlambat" otomatis.

## Common vaccination schedule in Indonesia
Follow the local livestock office / veterinarian. Typically: FMD (initial dose, booster after 4 weeks, then every 6 months); LSD (one dose, annual booster); Haemorrhagic septicaemia (annual, before the rainy season); Anthrax (annual in endemic areas); Brucellosis (young heifers under the government programme). Record each vaccination in the Terrava Health Records tab; the vaccination status turns "Due" or "Overdue" automatically.

## Pakan dan air / Feed and water
Sapi membutuhkan bahan kering sekitar 2,5–3% bobot badan per hari. Air minum: sapi potong 30–50 L/hari, sapi perah 80–120 L/hari, lebih banyak saat panas. Body Condition Score (BCS) ideal 3–3,5 dari skala 5.

Cattle need roughly 2.5–3% of body weight in dry matter per day. Water: beef cattle 30–50 L/day, dairy cows 80–120 L/day, more in hot weather. Ideal Body Condition Score is 3–3.5 on a 5-point scale.

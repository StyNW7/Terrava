# Penyakit umum dan tindakan awal / Common diseases and first actions

## Demam dan infeksi
Tanda: suhu > 39,5 °C, detak jantung naik, nafsu makan dan aktivitas turun, hidung kering, telinga dingin/panas. Tindakan awal: pisahkan ternak, pastikan air minum dan peneduh, catat suhu tiap 4–6 jam, hubungi dokter hewan bila suhu > 40 °C atau berlangsung lebih dari 24 jam. Jangan memberi antibiotik tanpa resep dokter hewan karena ada masa henti (withdrawal) susu dan daging.

## Fever and infection
Signs: temperature above 39.5 °C, raised heart rate, reduced appetite and activity, dry muzzle. First actions: separate the animal, ensure water and shade, record temperature every 4–6 hours, and call the veterinarian if it exceeds 40 °C or lasts more than 24 hours. Never give antibiotics without a veterinary prescription because milk and meat withdrawal periods apply.

## Penyakit Mulut dan Kuku (PMK / FMD)
Penyakit virus sangat menular, wajib lapor. Tanda: demam tinggi, air liur berlebihan, lepuh di mulut, lidah, kuku dan puting, pincang. Tindakan: isolasi segera, laporkan ke dinas peternakan/puskeswan setempat, batasi lalu lintas orang dan kendaraan, desinfeksi kandang. Pencegahan utama adalah vaksinasi PMK.

## Foot-and-Mouth Disease (FMD)
A highly contagious, notifiable viral disease. Signs: high fever, heavy drooling, blisters on the mouth, tongue, feet and teats, lameness. Actions: isolate immediately, report to the local livestock office/animal health centre, restrict movement of people and vehicles, disinfect the barn. Vaccination is the main prevention.

## Lumpy Skin Disease (LSD)
Ditularkan serangga (lalat, nyamuk, caplak). Tanda: demam, benjolan keras 2–5 cm di kulit, pembengkakan kelenjar getah bening, produksi susu turun. Tindakan: isolasi, kendalikan vektor serangga, laporkan ke dinas, vaksinasi LSD.

Spread by insects (flies, mosquitoes, ticks). Signs: fever, firm 2–5 cm skin nodules, swollen lymph nodes, falling milk yield. Actions: isolate, control insects, report to the authorities, vaccinate against LSD.

## Gangguan pernapasan (BRD / pneumonia)
Tanda: napas cepat atau berat, batuk, ingus, SpO2 < 92%, demam. Faktor risiko: transportasi, ventilasi buruk, kepadatan tinggi, perubahan cuaca. Tindakan: pindahkan ke kandang berventilasi baik, hubungi dokter hewan, pantau ternak sekandang.

Respiratory disease (BRD / pneumonia) signs: rapid or laboured breathing, coughing, nasal discharge, SpO2 below 92%, fever. Risk factors: transport, poor ventilation, crowding, weather swings. Move the animal to a well-ventilated pen, call the veterinarian, and watch pen-mates.

## Mastitis
Radang ambing pada sapi perah. Tanda: ambing bengkak, panas, nyeri; susu menggumpal atau berubah warna; kadang demam. Tindakan: uji CMT, perah tuntas, jaga kebersihan puting (dipping), konsultasi dokter hewan untuk pengobatan.

Udder inflammation in dairy cows. Signs: swollen, hot, painful udder; clotted or discoloured milk; sometimes fever. Actions: run a CMT test, milk out fully, teat dipping hygiene, and consult the veterinarian for treatment.

## Pincang / Lameness
Tanda: aktivitas dan jumlah langkah turun tajam tanpa demam, enggan berdiri, berjalan pincang. Penyebab: luka kuku, laminitis, lantai licin atau kasar. Tindakan: periksa dan bersihkan kuku, potong kuku berkala, perbaiki lantai, istirahatkan ternak.

Signs: sharp drop in activity and steps without fever, reluctance to stand, uneven gait. Causes: hoof injuries, laminitis, slippery or rough floors. Actions: inspect and clean hooves, regular hoof trimming, fix flooring, rest the animal.

## Milk fever (hipokalsemia) / metabolic disorders
Biasanya 0–3 hari setelah melahirkan. Tanda: suhu di bawah normal, lemah, tidak mau berdiri, telinga dingin. Ini darurat: hubungi dokter hewan untuk infus kalsium. Pencegahan: manajemen pakan masa kering.

Usually 0–3 days after calving. Signs: below-normal temperature, weakness, unable to stand, cold ears. This is an emergency: call the veterinarian for calcium therapy. Prevent with dry-period ration management.
